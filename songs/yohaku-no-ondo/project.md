# 余白の温度

## 基本資料

- Slug：yohaku-no-ondo
- 語言：Japanese
- BPM：86
- 拍號：4/4
- 調性與和聲：bittersweet minor, suspended piano harmony, subtle chromatic descent, warm major-color chorus; original progression

## 致敬來源

- 參考歌曲：Lemon
- 參考歌手：米津玄師 Kenshi Yonezu（僅作研究辨識，不送入 Suno）

## 致敬目的

研究中速日系抒情流行如何以克制的主歌、微妙切分節奏、鋼琴與木吉他、逐步擴張的弦樂，以及苦甜和聲承載失去之後難以消散的記憶。重新詮釋為搬離共同住處的最後一天，敘事者從牆上的家具痕跡與空房回音，理解缺席也會留下具體的溫度。

## 原創界線

- 全新日文歌詞、故事、旋律、Hook、和弦進行與節奏動機
- 只保留中速苦甜抒情、克制敘事與逐步擴張編曲等抽象特徵
- 不引用或改寫原詞句子、語序、押韻與副歌結構
- 避免夢、檸檬氣味、雨停、光、輪廓、切半果實等原作標誌意象
- 不重現參考歌曲的旋律、前奏、鼓點、和弦配置或人聲唱腔
- 不模仿特定歌手本人聲線

## 歌詞精神

故事發生在搬離共同住處的最後一天。紙箱已經封好，家具搬走後，牆面留下顏色深淺不同的長方形，地板也保留椅腳的位置。敘事者沒有試圖把失去變成答案，只是在逐間房間關門時，承認那個人曾經真實改變自己的生活。

核心比喻是「余白の温度」：空白不是遺忘，而是曾經被某人占據過的證據。情緒從機械式整理、壓抑回想，發展到允許悲傷存在，最後帶走的不是遺物，而是學會為缺席保留位置。副歌應簡潔、易記、帶長音，但不使用原作的感官象徵與「你仍是我的某種指引」式結論。

## 曲風

- bittersweet Japanese piano pop ballad
- organic-electronic chamber pop

## 聲響與年代

- modern Japanese pop, intimate and autumnal
- restrained verses, quietly cinematic choruses

## 樂器搭配

- acoustic piano｜broken chords and suspended voicings
- fingerpicked acoustic guitar｜soft pulse and human texture
- hybrid drums｜dry kick, brushed snare, subtle electronic clicks
- round electric bass｜restrained syncopation and warm sustain
- chamber strings｜low counterlines, widening final chorus
- clean electric guitar｜distant volume-swells and short replies

## 演唱方式

- youthful Japanese female lead, clear, intimate and naturally breathy
- low conversational verses, controlled emotional lift and sustained chorus notes; no singer imitation

## 製作方式

- close dry verse vocal, warm short plate in choruses
- organic dynamics, clear piano, soft low end, subtle stereo room

## 情緒曲線

### Intro

- 強度：2
- 發展：four-bar original piano figure with room noise and one distant guitar swell

### Verse 1

- 強度：3
- 發展：voice, piano and tiny electronic clicks describe packing the final boxes without naming the loss directly

### Pre-Chorus

- 強度：5
- 發展：bass and acoustic guitar enter as empty marks on the wall trigger a precise memory

### Chorus

- 強度：7
- 發展：restrained full drums and chamber strings open around the original hook about warmth remaining inside blank space

### Verse 2

- 強度：4
- 發展：pull back to a warmer groove; ordinary sounds reveal how shared habits have survived

### Bridge

- 強度：6
- 發展：piano and female vocal become exposed, accepting that grief does not need to be solved

### Final Chorus

- 強度：9
- 發展：widest strings, firmer drums and subtle octave harmony; sorrow turns into permission to continue

### Outro

- 強度：2
- 發展：return to piano as the last door closes, ending on a suspended but peaceful chord

## Suno 額外要求

- original Japanese melody with natural prosody and a memorable restrained chorus
- immediate intimate opening, no long ambient intro
- about four minutes
- keep the first verse sparse and save the widest strings for the final chorus
- emotional but never melodramatic; preserve breaths and small silences

## Exclude

- artist voice imitation
- existing melody, lyric, chord or rhythmic quotation
- direct artist or song-title reference in generated lyrics
- lemon, citrus, fruit, rain, dream or guiding-light imagery
- male lead vocal
- anime character voice
- power ballad belting
- excessive vibrato or melisma
- trap hi-hat rolls
- EDM drop
- lo-fi beat tape
- retro funk city pop
- distorted rock wall
- huge cinematic percussion
- gospel choir
- vocal chops
- hyper-compressed master
