# 逆光シグナル

## 基本資料

- Slug：gyakkou-signal
- 語言：Japanese
- BPM：132
- 拍號：4/4
- 調性與和聲：minor-key verses with rapid diatonic motion, suspended tension and a bright relative-major lift in the chorus; fully original chord progression and melody

## 致敬來源

- 參考歌曲：夜に駆ける
- 參考歌手：YOASOBI（僅作研究辨識，不送入 Suno）

## 致敬目的

研究高速鋼琴驅動的現代日系敘事流行、密集歌詞與明暗急轉的情緒推進，重新詮釋為兩個被城市通知與期待壓得喘不過氣的人，在第一班電車到來前關掉手機，選擇照自己的節奏前進。

## 原創界線

- 全新歌詞、故事、旋律、和弦進行與 Hook
- 只保留高速敘事、明暗反差、鋼琴與電子節奏交織等抽象特徵
- 不引用參考歌詞的標誌句、意象排列或段落旋律
- 不重現參考歌曲的前奏、主旋律、低音線或節奏型
- 不模仿特定歌手聲線

## 歌詞精神

凌晨零點過後，手機通知、末班車廣播與商店招牌仍在替所有人規定下一步。敘事者遇見同樣厭倦被安排的對方，兩人不是逃向毀滅，而是暫時關掉訊息，跑過尚未醒來的街區，在天色變白前重新選擇自己的速度。

核心比喻是「逆光中的信號」：看不清終點，仍能辨認彼此給出的微弱方向。情緒由窒息、猶疑與自責，轉為互相允許停頓，最後在晨光中把奔跑改寫為主動生活。副歌 Hook 必須簡潔、可唱，但不可使用參考曲的夜晚墜落、融化或牽手奔馳等標誌表達。

## 曲風

- fast piano-driven Japanese narrative pop
- electronic pop rock with orchestral accents
- high-energy nocturnal city pop

## 聲響與年代

- polished contemporary Japanese pop
- urgent, luminous and bittersweet
- dense forward motion with sharp light-and-shadow contrast
- cinematic city-at-midnight atmosphere without retro city-pop styling

## 樂器搭配

- bright acoustic piano｜rapid staccato arpeggios, syncopated chord jabs and short ascending fills; original motifs only
- tight electronic-acoustic drum kit｜punchy kick, crisp snare, fast closed hi-hats and brief tom lifts into choruses
- picked electric bass｜nimble melodic movement that locks tightly with the kick without copying any existing bass line
- layered analog synth arpeggio｜subtle sixteenth-note propulsion beneath the piano
- muted clean electric guitar｜percussive offbeat accents and small answering figures
- compact string ensemble｜short rhythmic ostinatos in transitions and wider sustained lines in the final chorus
- airy synth pad｜thin nocturnal atmosphere in verses, opening into brighter stereo width
- reverse cymbals and filtered noise risers｜quick sectional transitions, never forming an EDM drop

## 演唱方式

- clear agile Japanese female lead in a bright upper-middle register
- precise consonants and quick syllabic phrasing during verses
- compact breath placement that keeps dense lines intelligible
- pre-choruses tighten emotionally before an open, soaring but controlled chorus
- natural youthful tone without impersonating any identifiable singer
- restrained harmony doubles in the first chorus, wider thirds and octave support only in the final chorus

## 製作方式

- close centered vocal with short bright plate reverb and subtle tempo delay
- crisp piano transients placed slightly wide around the vocal
- tight controlled low end with bass articulation preserved
- energetic but breathable master; keep sectional contrast and micro-pauses
- verses relatively narrow, choruses wider through strings, synth layers and backing vocals
- clean modern mix with no exaggerated sub-bass or harsh top-end

## 情緒曲線

### Intro

- 強度：4
- 發展：a two-bar original piano cell starts immediately, joined by a filtered pulse and a fast drum pickup

### Verse 1

- 強度：5
- 發展：dense close-mic narration over clipped piano, bass and tight hats; the city feels mechanically scheduled

### Pre-Chorus

- 強度：7
- 發展：harmony climbs, drums briefly thin out and the vocal becomes more urgent as both characters decide to disconnect

### Chorus

- 強度：9
- 發展：full drums, wide piano, synth and strings release into a bright original hook about choosing the wind over notifications

### Post-Chorus

- 強度：7
- 發展：short piano-and-synth answer motif, brisk and memorable without a vocal chop

### Verse 2

- 強度：6
- 發展：retain the pulse while adding muted guitar; doubts and self-criticism interrupt the escape

### Bridge

- 強度：4
- 發展：half-time feel, piano and voice exposed; the pair gives each other permission to stop and breathe

### Build

- 強度：8
- 發展：snare lift, rising strings and increasingly dense syllables lead straight into the last refrain

### Final Chorus

- 強度：10
- 發展：widest arrangement and upper harmony support; the night gives way to a self-chosen morning, followed by a brief stop before the last line

### Outro

- 強度：3
- 發展：drums cut away, leaving the original piano cell and distant station ambience implied through synth texture

## Suno 額外要求

- immediate piano-led opening with no long ambient intro
- original Japanese topline and a concise memorable chorus hook
- brisk narrative verses with natural Japanese prosody
- multiple small arrangement changes every four to eight bars
- about three minutes forty seconds
- short instrumental turnaround after the first chorus
- final chorus should feel hopeful and self-directed rather than tragic

## Exclude

- artist voice imitation
- existing melody or lyric quotation
- recognizable intro or bass-line recreation
- direct reference to any artist or song title in the generated lyrics
- suicidal or self-destructive ending
- EDM festival drop
- trap hi-hat rolls
- retro funk city pop
- heavy metal guitars
- acoustic ballad pacing
- vocal chops
- excessive melisma
- huge cinematic percussion
- gospel choir
- muddy piano
- hyper-compressed master
