# ノイズの花

## 基本資料

- Slug：noise-no-hana
- 語言：Japanese
- BPM：128
- 拍號：4/4
- 調性與和聲：dark minor, chromatic bass tension, brighter defiant chorus; original progression

## 致敬來源

- 參考歌曲：怪物
- 參考歌手：YOASOBI（僅作研究辨識，不送入 Suno）

## 致敬目的

研究帶有危險感的高速日系電子流行、切分低音、銳利鼓組、戲劇化段落切換，以及年輕女聲在低語與爆發之間的張力。重新詮釋為一座以分數決定價值的城市中，敘事者撕掉「合格」標籤，替被系統消音的朋友奪回名字與聲音。

## 原創界線

- 全新歌詞、故事、旋律、Hook、和弦進行與節奏動機
- 只採用黑暗動能、切分節奏、密集敘事與爆發副歌等抽象特徵
- 不引用參考歌詞的標誌句、問答結構、捕食意象或守護宣言
- 不重現參考歌曲的低音線、銅管動機、前奏、旋律或段落節拍配置
- 不模仿特定歌手本人聲線

## 歌詞精神

城市每天替人們更新分數，低於標準的名字會從看板、課堂與群組裡逐漸消失。敘事者原本努力維持綠色的「合格」標誌，直到朋友的聲音被判定為雜訊。她第一次公開破壞規則，把對方留下的失真錄音送上全城廣播。

核心比喻是「噪音開花」：被排除的聲音並非錯誤，而是新秩序的種子。情緒由冷眼服從、秘密懷疑，推進到承認自己的膽怯，最後不是變得更強或擊敗某人，而是讓許多不同聲音同時存在。避免野獸、吞噬、黑眼睛、奔跑守護與反覆追問身分等原作辨識度高的語意。

## 曲風

- dark kinetic Japanese electro-pop
- syncopated electronic pop rock

## 聲響與年代

- sleek contemporary J-pop, dystopian neon tension
- restless, gritty and theatrical with sudden negative space

## 樂器搭配

- distorted synth bass｜syncopated chromatic groove
- hybrid electronic drums｜dry kick, metallic snare, shuffled hats
- percussive piano｜low stabs and tense clusters
- glitch synth plucks｜offbeat answers
- low synth brass｜dissonant accents
- muted electric guitar｜scraped chorus pulses

## 演唱方式

- youthful Japanese female lead, clear, agile, naturally edgy
- precise rapid verses, open controlled high chorus; no singer imitation

## 製作方式

- dry close verse vocal, wider dark chorus
- tight kick and bass, brief dropouts, gritty stereo glitches
- clear diction and dynamic master

## 情緒曲線

### Intro

- 強度：6
- 發展：an original distorted bass pulse and metallic click establish the groove immediately, followed by one dissonant piano stab

### Verse 1

- 強度：5
- 發展：dry rapid narration over bass, kick and sparse glitch plucks; the scoring system appears polished but oppressive

### Pre-Chorus

- 強度：7
- 發展：harmony tightens, synth lead rises and drum accents become less predictable as the friend is erased

### Chorus

- 強度：9
- 發展：wide drums, bass, guitar and synth brass release around a fully original hook about noise blooming through concrete

### Verse 2

- 強度：6
- 發展：return with extra piano punctuation and denser hats; the narrator admits having benefited from silence

### Breakdown

- 強度：3
- 發展：near-spoken vocal, filtered room tone and a fragment of distorted recorded speech; shame becomes a decision

### Bridge

- 強度：7
- 發展：half-time kick and rising string tremolo support a clear public refusal of the city's ranking language

### Build

- 強度：9
- 發展：rapid vocal phrases, snare acceleration and chromatic bass ascent lead into a half-beat silence

### Final Chorus

- 強度：10
- 發展：fullest stereo image, octave vocal doubles and counterlines; many excluded voices join as texture without becoming a choir

### Outro

- 強度：4
- 發展：the groove breaks into irregular clicks while one clean human voice remains, followed by a dry final bass note

## Suno 額外要求

- start immediately with an original syncopated bass groove, no ambient prelude
- fully original Japanese melody with a short defiant chorus hook
- young female lead, agile low verses and controlled high chorus
- intricate rhythmic arrangement with brief dropouts and frequent four-bar variations
- about three minutes thirty seconds
- second verse should intensify rather than simply repeat the first
- dark and confrontational atmosphere resolving into collective liberation, not violence

## Exclude

- artist voice imitation
- existing melody, lyric, bass-line or brass-motif quotation
- direct reference to any artist or song title in generated lyrics
- beast, monster, predator or devouring imagery
- violent revenge narrative
- anime character voice
- male lead vocal
- trap hi-hat rolls
- dubstep wobble bass
- EDM festival drop
- retro funk city pop
- metal screaming
- orchestral trailer percussion
- gospel choir
- vocal chops as the main hook
- excessive melisma
- muddy low end
- hyper-compressed master
