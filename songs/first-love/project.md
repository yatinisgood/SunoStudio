# First Love

## 基本資料

- Slug：first-love
- 語言：Mandarin Chinese with a short English hook
- BPM：82
- 拍號：4/4
- 調性與和聲：minor-leaning verses opening into a bittersweet major-colored chorus

## 致敬來源

- 參考歌曲：First Love
- 參考歌手：宇多田光（僅作研究辨識，不送入 Suno）

## 致敬目的

致敬初戀結束多年後仍留在心中的溫度；改寫成成熟後回望、感謝而不挽留的故事。

## 原創界線

- 全新中文敘事
- 全新旋律與 Hook
- 不引用原歌詞
- 不模仿原唱聲線

## 歌詞精神

保留「多年後仍記得初戀」的情感核心，但不描寫等待復合。

> 初戀留下的不是遺憾，而是日後懂得去愛的能力。

故事從多年後整理舊物開始。主角已經繼續生活，最後選擇感謝而不是追回。核心比喻是「第一束光」：光仍留在生命裡，但對方不需要回來。

必須避免逐句翻譯參考歌詞、沿用原作英文 Hook、複製標誌性意象，以及把結局寫成永遠無法愛上別人。

## 曲風

- late-1990s inspired R&B ballad
- piano-led pop soul

## 聲響與年代

- warm analog nostalgia
- restrained cinematic atmosphere

## 樂器搭配

- acoustic piano｜intimate broken chords and the harmonic anchor
- warm electric piano｜soft R&B color beneath the verses
- fingered electric bass｜sparse, rounded movement that grows in the chorus
- restrained live drums｜brush-like verses, fuller snare and cymbals near the climax
- string ensemble｜long lines entering gradually after the first chorus
- clean electric guitar｜occasional answering phrases, never dominant

## 演唱方式

- intimate lower-register lead vocal
- breathy but natural phrasing
- controlled verses growing into an open emotional final chorus
- subtle harmony stacks only in later choruses

## 製作方式

- close vocal with warm plate reverb
- wide strings but centered rhythm section
- organic dynamics
- gentle tape-like saturation
- clear piano transients

## 情緒曲線

### Intro

- 強度：1
- 發展：solo piano, hesitant and spacious

### Verse 1

- 強度：2
- 發展：private recollection, nearly conversational

### Pre-Chorus

- 強度：4
- 發展：bass and harmony begin to move, restrained anticipation

### Chorus

- 強度：6
- 發展：drums and strings open the room; bittersweet release rather than power belting

### Verse 2

- 強度：3
- 發展：pull back while retaining a quiet pulse

### Bridge

- 強度：7
- 發展：harmonic lift and honest realization, strings rising

### Final Chorus

- 強度：9
- 發展：fullest arrangement and layered harmonies, then release

### Outro

- 強度：2
- 發展：return to piano and one unresolved afterglow

## Suno 額外要求

- memorable original chorus hook
- emotionally precise pauses
- about four minutes
- no direct melodic quotation

## Exclude

- artist voice imitation
- existing melody quotation
- trap hi-hats
- EDM drop
- aggressive rock guitars
- oversized gospel choir
- vocal chopping
- excessive melisma
- hyper-compressed master
- cheerful bubblegum pop
