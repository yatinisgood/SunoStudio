# その日まで

## 基本資料

- Slug：sono-hi-made
- 語言：Japanese
- BPM：74
- 拍號：4/4
- 調性與和聲：warm major-key folk-pop harmony with bittersweet suspended tones, open fifths and gentle pentatonic color

## 致敬來源

- 參考歌曲：それぞれに（各自遠揚）
- 參考歌手：中孝介（僅作研究辨識，不送入 Suno）

## 致敬目的

致敬離開故鄉、各自走向不同道路時的離愁與祝福。重新詮釋為隔著海與季節仍替對方守望，約定在各自成長後微笑重逢的故事。

## 原創界線

- 允許保留送別、遠行、祝福與再會等可辨識的情感精神
- 允許短句與意象形成有限度的致敬呼應
- 使用全新旋律、段落發展與主要 Hook
- 不整段複製參考歌詞
- 不模仿特定歌手本人聲線

## 歌詞精神

這不是絕望的分手歌，而是一首把不捨轉化成祝福的送別歌。

> 即使我們走向不同的天空，只要仍相信彼此的夢，總有一天能帶著笑容再會。

故事以海風、春光、夕暮、潮聲與遙遠天空串起季節。敘事者留在原地或踏上另一條路，對遠方的人表達牽掛，但不要求對方回頭。情緒從安靜的不捨逐步打開，最後落在「その日まで」的溫柔承諾。

## 曲風

- Japanese acoustic folk-pop ballad
- Amami shima-uta-influenced contemporary pop
- cinematic farewell song

## 聲響與年代

- mid-2000s organic Japanese pop
- warm coastal atmosphere with generous space
- nostalgic but quietly hopeful
- traditional island-song emotion expressed through modern acoustic production

## 樂器搭配

- warm acoustic piano｜slow open arpeggios and the main harmonic anchor
- acoustic guitar｜gentle fingerpicking and light strums that suggest forward motion
- round electric bass｜simple sustained notes, entering gradually after the first verse
- restrained live drums｜soft kick, brushed snare and sparse cymbal swells
- legato string ensemble｜long supportive lines that widen the chorus without becoming grandiose
- subtle bamboo flute｜brief answering phrases in the intro and interlude, airy rather than ornamental
- ambient coastal texture｜very faint wind-like air and room resonance, never literal sound effects

## 演唱方式

- clear high-register Japanese male tenor
- gentle nasal and head-voice resonance influenced by Amami island folk singing
- smooth chest-to-falsetto transitions
- delicate upward scoops and controlled kobushi ornaments at phrase endings
- sustained open vowels with precise Japanese diction
- restrained emotional delivery that grows through resonance rather than belting

## 製作方式

- intimate centered vocal with natural room and warm plate reverb
- organic acoustic dynamics with minimal compression
- piano and vocal remain clear at the center
- strings widen gradually from the first chorus onward
- soft rounded low end with no modern sub-bass emphasis
- spacious ending with a gentle ritardando and long natural decay

### Intro

- 強度：1
- 發展：sparse piano and a distant airy melodic response, like wind crossing the sea

### Verse 1

- 強度：2
- 發展：intimate recollection of spring departure, vocal kept narrow and tender

### Pre-Chorus

- 強度：4
- 發展：acoustic guitar and bass add motion as the two paths begin to separate

### Chorus

- 強度：6
- 發展：strings open gently, high sustained vocal phrases turn sadness into blessing

### Verse 2

- 強度：3
- 發展：pull back slightly, introduce passing seasons and continued remembrance

### Bridge

- 強度：7
- 發展：most vulnerable moment, longer vocal ornaments over rising strings and harmonic suspension

### Final Chorus

- 強度：9
- 發展：fullest acoustic arrangement, resonant high register and subtle harmony support, hopeful rather than triumphant

### Outro

- 強度：2
- 發展：return to piano and air, gentle ritardando on the promise to meet again

## Suno 額外要求

- original Japanese melody with a memorable but understated chorus
- emotionally natural Japanese phrasing
- short breaths between lyrical images
- vocal ornaments concentrated at important phrase endings
- about four minutes
- preserve silence and acoustic dynamics

## Exclude

- artist voice imitation
- female lead vocal
- bright anime-pop vocal tone
- exaggerated enka vibrato
- excessive melisma on every line
- festival-style folk arrangement
- EDM drop
- trap hi-hats
- aggressive rock guitars
- huge cinematic percussion
- gospel choir
- heavy autotune
- hyper-compressed master
